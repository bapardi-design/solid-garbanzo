# Module 6 · Selling it as a service

*For Mediha and anyone reselling the Autopilot. This is the productised-service playbook.*

## What you're actually selling

Not software. **"Your social media runs in the background, and you approve every post."** The software is the reason you can promise that at a price a solo founder can pay. The value is the brand kit workshop (they get clarity they never had), the review habit (20 minutes a week instead of five hours), and the fact that it keeps going.

That maps neatly onto the same message as partner marketing: set it up once, it runs in the background, you keep control.

## The onboarding flow (2 weeks)

1. **Kick-off call (60 min):** send Module 0 beforehand. Go through voice, audience, pillars, offers. Record the call; the transcript is gold for `we_say`.
2. **Day 2–3:** you fill `brand.yaml`, `strategy.md`, adjust template colours. Generate three posts. Send screenshots.
3. **Feedback round (30 min call):** read captions together. Fix the kit, not the captions. Generate again.
4. **Day 5:** client connects Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn in Ayrshare (send them the Ayrshare link; 10 minutes), you set up Notion and GitHub.
5. **Day 7:** first real week generated. Review training on a screen share (Module 3). First post goes out with you watching.
6. **Week 2:** they review alone; you check the board once. Handover done.

## Objections you'll hear

- *"AI content sounds generic."* → Show three posts from a real kit next to their last three posts. The kit is the answer; that's why the workshop exists.
- *"I want to stay in control."* → Nothing posts without their click. Show the board.
- *"I don't want to learn a new tool."* → The tool is Notion (or a text file) and one dropdown.
- *"What about video?"* → Not in v1; carousels and stories are where saves and shares come from. Video slideshows are on the roadmap (Module 4).
- *"What if it breaks?"* → The card turns red with the reason; nothing silent. And you're on the monthly plan.

## The training you deliver

Modules 0 and 3 are the client-facing ones (25 minutes of reading + a 45-minute call). Modules 1, 2, 4, 5 are yours, or for a VA you train to run several clients. A VA can run ten clients in a few hours a week: plan topics monthly, watch the boards, port feedback into brand kits.

## Selling the system itself

If you want to sell the setup rather than run it, the product is: this repository as a template + Modules 0–5 as the course + a 2-hour setup call. Price it like a course with implementation (€900–1,500). Buyers need: a GitHub account, an Anthropic key, Ayrshare. Module 1 is the exact checklist.

## Positioning line

> Your social media, written in your voice, designed in your brand, posted on schedule. You approve every post in 20 minutes a week. Set it up once. It runs in the background.
