# Social media strategy — Female Signature Partnermarketing

> This file is read by the AI on every generation run. Keep it current; it is
> the "why" behind every post. The brand kit (`brand.yaml`) is the "how we sound".

## Goal (next 90 days)

1. Grow the email list via the free **Partner Marketing Guide** (primary CTA on ~70% of posts).
2. Book strategy calls with founders who are already spending on ads (secondary CTA, ~30%).
3. Establish Mediha as *the* voice for partner & creator marketing for female founders in DACH.

## Core message

Paid ads rent attention. Partner & creator marketing builds a channel you own:
real people recommending your product to people who trust them. Set it up once,
it runs in the background. Most female founders have never tried it.

## Audience journey

| Stage | What they think | What we post |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | "Ads are the only way to scale." | Myth-busting, ad-cost reality checks |
| Curious | "What is partner marketing exactly?" | Education carousels, definitions, step-by-steps |
| Evaluating | "Would this work for *my* brand?" | Proof, mini case studies, founder stories |
| Ready | "How do I start?" | Guide CTA, behind-the-scenes of the programme, call CTA |

## Channel roles

- **Instagram** — main channel. Carousels for education, singles for proof/mindset, stories for behind-the-scenes.
- **TikTok** — photo-mode carousels reusing the Instagram designs with shorter, more conversational captions.
- **LinkedIn** — same carousels, more professional captions, founder-to-founder tone.

## Cadence

- 4 posts / week (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri) on Instagram; each one cross-posted to TikTok and LinkedIn where the format fits.
- Weekly mix follows the pillar shares in `brand.yaml` (Education 40 / Proof 25 / Mindset 20 / Behind 15).

## Hooks that work for this audience

- A question that names the pain: "Still putting 80% of your budget into ads?"
- A contrast: "Ads: pay for clicks. Partners: pay for sales."
- A number: "3 partners brought in 41% of her revenue last quarter."
- A confession: "I ran ads for two years before I understood this."

## CTAs

- Freebie: *Comment GUIDE / DM me and I'll send you the free Partner Marketing Guide.*
- Call: *Book a free strategy call — link in bio.*
- Engagement: *Which of these have you tried?*

## KPIs we watch

Saves and shares on carousels, comments with "GUIDE", profile visits → link clicks, calls booked.

## Never

No income claims we can't back, no naming clients without permission, no "hustle" language.
